🚀 Just built SparkLens — an AI-powered Startup Idea Validator!

Ever had a startup idea but didn't know if it's worth pursuing? I built SparkLens to solve exactly that.

✨ What it does:
→ Paste your startup idea
→ AI analyzes it instantly
→ Get a professional report with SWOT analysis, market scoring, risk assessment, and 5 actionable next steps
→ Download as PDF
→ All saved to history automatically — no login needed

🛠 Tech Stack:
• Django 4.2 (Backend)
• OpenRouter AI / GPT (Intelligence)
• WeasyPrint (PDF Export)
• WhiteNoise + Gunicorn (Production)
• Rich white UI with bold animations
• SQLite (Zero-config database)

🎯 Key Features:
✅ Session-based history (14-day persistence, no signup)
✅ Animated SVG score rings
✅ Staggered entrance animations
✅ Professional PDF export with watermark
✅ Production-ready deployment config
✅ Clean architecture — AI logic fully separated from views

💡 Why I built it:
Too many founders waste months building ideas nobody wants. SparkLens gives honest, structured validation in seconds — for free.
